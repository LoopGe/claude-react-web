Liquid Glass

Edge-weighted refraction · specular rim · drifting sheen

88 t/s
Liquid Glass refracts light most strongly at its curved edges, while the center stays optically clear — exactly how a real glass lens bends the world behind it.

Uses an edge-weighted displacement map in <feDisplacementMap> via backdrop-filter. Refraction concentrates at the bezel (adjustable). Chrome / Edge only — Safari/Firefox fall back to a frosted blur.
